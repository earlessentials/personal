A little piece of my heart

A thought from the creator

For the part of you that still believes.

I've always liked the idea that a normal day can become a memory we carry for the rest of our lives. A conversation we nearly missed, a place we spontaneously decided to visit, a stranger who became someone important, or a small decision that changed the direction of our lives.

As we grow older, I think many of us become accustomed to knowing what comes next. We plan our days, settle into familiar routines, and occasionally wonder why time seems to pass so quickly. I sometimes wonder if it has something to do with how few things we experience for the first time.

I miss the anticipation I felt as a child, when waking up on a particular morning felt special, when curiosity was reason enough to do something, and when the smallest things could give me something to look forward to.

That feeling became the starting point for PLOT TWIST.

I wanted to create something that brings a little surprise into everyday life, giving you something to look forward to throughout December.

In a world where almost everything is available to us immediately, I wanted to bring back the simple pleasure of anticipation, of wondering what tomorrow might bring and discovering things one day at a time.

PLOT TWIST is also a personal reminder that we can carry our sense of wonder into adulthood. We can still be playful, get excited about small things, and make ordinary days feel special simply because we want to.

As the year comes to an end, I hope this experience brings a little joy into your daily life. I hope opening each door makes you smile, sparks your curiosity, and reminds you of a part of yourself you may have forgotten over the years.

By the time you open the final door, I hope you'll find a few more things to appreciate about the life you're already living.

After all, every good story deserves a little plot twist.

With love,Pearling
