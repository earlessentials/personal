PRIVACY POLICY

Your conversation
belongs to you.

Last updated: 17 November 2026

E—— Deep! is a conversation game by Pearling Lim. Here is how this version of the website handles information.

Your game session

The game does not ask you to type your answers, record conversations, or save reflections. Your spoken answers are not collected by the game. The names you enter, your solo/partner/group choice, the card you are viewing, and the cards already drawn are held temporarily in this tab’s memory to personalize your welcome and avoid repeats. This game state resets when you reload or close the page.

The game code does not use browser cookies, local storage, or analytics scripts to track your play.

The invitation password

Your invitation password is used in this browser to unlock an encrypted card deck. The game does not send the password to a sign-in service or save it in browser storage. Reloading or closing the page clears the unlocked session and player names.

Services that deliver the site

The website loads its typefaces from Google Fonts. Loading the website and its fonts sends ordinary web requests to third-party services. Their infrastructure may process technical information, such as IP addresses and browser details, to deliver and protect their services. Their own privacy practices apply to that processing.

Any sign-in or access checks required by the hosting platform are handled by that platform, separately from the game.

Links and getting in touch

Private Matter is an exclusive publication by Pearling. You can access it by subscribing or reading its open articles. Its own privacy practices apply when you visit. Selecting the email link opens your email application; it does not send a message automatically.

If you email hi@pearlinglim.com, you share your email address and whatever you include in your message with Pearling for the purpose of responding to you.

Questions and updates

This policy describes the current website and may be updated if its features or services change. The date above identifies the latest revision.

For privacy questions, contact hi@pearlinglim.com.
