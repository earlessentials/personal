02 / THE FRAMEWORK

Three pillars.
Crystal-clear storytelling.

A strong foundation. The right words.
A meaningful next step.

PILLAR ONE

Structure

A strong foundation guides the reader effortlessly.

  • Start strong. Hook them from the first line. Explore the examples in the Hook Hub.
  • Logical flow. Each sentence leads naturally to the next. No head-scratching allowed.
  • Clear takeaways. What do you want the reader to remember? Make it obvious.
PILLAR TWO

Language

The words you choose make all the difference.

  • Strong verbs. Active voice is your friend. It creates energy and clarity.
  • Specific nouns. “Car” is vague. “Cherry-red vintage convertible” paints a picture.
  • Sensory details. Engage the reader’s senses to bring your story to life.
PILLAR THREE

Storyselling

The secret sauce: clarity isn’t just about what you say, but how you connect it to the sale.

  • Problem, Agitation, Solution. Define the problem, agitate the pain points, and present your solution as the hero.
  • Features tell, benefits sell. Show how they improve the reader’s life.
  • Call to action. Be crystal clear about what you want them to do next.
